How to get your cookies:

  1. Open claude.ai/settings/usage in your browser
  2. Make sure you're logged in and can see your usage
  3. Press F12 to open Developer Tools
  4. Click the Console tab
  5. Type document.cookie and press Enter
  6. Copy the ENTIRE output (it's a long string)
  7. Paste it in the box below and click Save

⚠️ Important: Make sure the output includes cf_clearance cookie or it won't work!
